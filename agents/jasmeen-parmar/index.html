How Jasmeen works

One agent, start to keys

As Principal Broker, Jasmeen is the licensed head of the brokerage: every file placed by every agent is reviewed under this licence before it goes to a lender, and every disclosure the regulator requires is signed off here. A pre-approval within one business day, a rate held for up to 120 days, one application presented to the lenders it fits best, and the same person on the phone from the first call to the day the keys change hands.

On most residential mortgages the advice costs the client nothing; the lender pays the brokerage. Where a fee applies, it is disclosed in writing on the regulator's form before anything is signed.
