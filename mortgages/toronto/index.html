What most people learn too late

01
Two land transfer taxes

Toronto charges its own land transfer tax on top of Ontario’s, so closing costs run higher than in the cities around it.

Land transfer tax calculator →
02
First-time buyers get money back

Up to $4,000 from Ontario and up to $4,475 from Toronto, for first-time buyers who qualify.

First-time buyers →
03
Condo fees count

Lenders count part of your monthly condo fees when they work out what you can borrow, so the fee changes how much you qualify for.

How much can I afford →
04
Prices change every month

The Toronto Regional Real Estate Board publishes what homes sold for, neighbourhood by neighbourhood, every quarter.

TRREB Market Watch →

Common questions

Buying in Toronto, answered plainly

Does Toronto have its own land transfer tax?

Yes. Toronto charges a municipal land transfer tax on top of Ontario’s. First-time buyers who qualify can get up to $4,475 of it back, and up to $4,000 of Ontario’s.

Do condo fees change how much I can borrow?

Yes. Lenders count part of the monthly fee with your other housing costs, so two condos at the same price can qualify differently.

How much do I need to put down?

The same as anywhere in Canada: 5% of the first $500,000 and 10% of the rest, up to $1.5 million. Our first-time buyers page has the full table.

Can I meet you in person?

Yes, at our Mississauga office on Derry Road East or our Brampton office on Goreway Drive. Office hours are on our contact page.
