Common questions

Buying in Mississauga, answered plainly

Does Mississauga have its own land transfer tax?

No. You pay Ontario’s land transfer tax only, and first-time buyers can get up to $4,000 of it back.

Do condo fees change how much I can borrow?

Yes. Lenders count part of the monthly fee with your other housing costs, so two condos at the same price can qualify differently.

How much do I need to put down?

The same as anywhere in Canada: 5% of the first $500,000 and 10% of the rest, up to $1.5 million. Our first-time buyers page has the full table.

Can I meet you in person?

Yes, at our Mississauga office on Derry Road East or our Brampton office on Goreway Drive. Office hours are on our contact page.
